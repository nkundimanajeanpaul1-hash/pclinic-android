🛡️ Privacy notice

Your patient records never leave this device. PClinic Pro has no server. Everything you enter is stored only in this app's private storage on this phone.

What is stored

  • Patient details, clinical notes, photos and videos.
  • Your account: username, and a password that is hashed — we never store the password itself.
  • A record of actions (sign-ins, edits, exports) shown under Activities.

How it is protected

  • Patient data is encrypted on this device with a key derived from your password.
  • Photos and videos are stored as encrypted files outside the app's settings storage.
  • Nobody at PClinic can read your records, and there is no account on a server to hack, because there is no server.

Two things you should know

  • A recovery e-mail address and phone number, if you set them, are kept unencrypted on this device. They have to be readable before you sign in, otherwise you could never reset a forgotten password. You can delete them at any time in Profile and rely on your recovery code instead.
  • If you lose your password and have no recovery code, e-mail or phone on the account, the encrypted data cannot be recovered and will have to be erased.

Backups are your responsibility. Exports you create live wherever you save them. Use a password when exporting — an unprotected backup file is readable by anyone who obtains it.

We collect nothing. No analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers. Reset codes are delivered by EmailJS, so that provider temporarily sees the address the code is sent to.
